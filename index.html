What awaits

Core Features

NT:NH is about building factories. Complex ones. The kind where you trace a production line back through a dozen machines and realize you need to redesign three of them. Built first for newcomers to HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod, the questbook guides you through every system step by step. Veterans will find enough depth to stay engaged.

Deep Machine Tech Tree

Work through alloy furnaces, chemical factories, centrifuges, assembly lines, and multiblock reactors. Every machine unlocks new possibilities in the chain.

Power Systems

Scale your energy production as your factory grows. Steam turbines get you started. Later you move into fission and fusion. Each tier demands more planning and infrastructure than the last.

Chemical Processing

Synthesize advanced alloys and compounds through a realistic chemical processing chain. Extract, refine, and react materials to produce components unavailable any other way.

Hazard Management

Some materials are dangerous. Suit up, handle them correctly, and route them into the right machines. Mismanage them and you'll feel it. Handle them well and they become valuable factory inputs.

300+ Guided Quests

A structured questbook walks you from first tools to late-game engineering, with logical branching paths, lore, and rewards that keep progression meaningful.

True Difficulty

Nutritional hunger system, smarter combat AI, and punishing boss encounters. Mistakes have real consequences - and victories feel earned.

Space Exploration

The endgame reaches beyond Minecraft's sky. New dimensions and challenges wait for players who have built and automated their way through everything below.

Optimized Performance

Angelica rendering engine and ArchaicFix keep the 1.7.10 experience smooth even on modest hardware. No sacrifices to visual quality required.

Active Development

Monthly releases, community-driven bug reports, and an open GitHub organization. The modpack grows with its players and their feedback.

⚙️ Built for Engineers

NT:NH rewards players who think in systems. Your assembly line needs parts. Parts need refined materials. Refining needs power. Power needs fuel. Fuel needs its own production line. Plan the whole chain or fall behind.

🧬 Inspired by GTNH

The name is no coincidence. GregTech: New Horizons (GT:NH) built its identity around GregTech. Nuclear Tech: New Horizons (NT:NH) does the same for HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod. Same philosophy of deep, structured progression. Entirely different mod at its core.

🎯 Who Is This For?

NT:NH is primarily built for newcomers to HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod. The 300+ quest questbook walks you through every system step by step, so you're never left guessing what to build next. Veteran NTM players will also find plenty to work through: deep automation layers, late-game machine chains, and a demanding progression that holds up even if you already know the mod well.
